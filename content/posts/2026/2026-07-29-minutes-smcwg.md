6.            Discussion
 
    *  Stephen Davidson noted that Ballot SMC017v2 was in IPR through July 30. He noted this ballot had early impact, requiring new S/MIME roots and ICAs created starting September 15 to be at least 4096 if using RSA.  Ashish Dhiman noted that NIST standards were also emphasizing stepup of RSA key sizes, and said that a future ballot might consider stepping up the minimum RSA key size for leaf certificates. 
    *  Stephen also noted changes that were taking effect in CCADB that were relevant to CA members.
    *  The WG reviewed the draft Ballot SMC018: Realignment of Multipurpose use cases.  See https://github.com/cabforum/smime/pull/306/changes.  Stephen noted that the item related to the CCADB Section 6.3 would be effective immediately, and that other changes would take effect on July 1, 2027 which corresponded to the matching requirements in the Apple Policy v2, whose release is imminent.  He thanked Apple for the early exposure to the draft of those changes, which had the impact of truncating in some cases the legacy S/MIME practices overlapping with other use cases like document signing.  Following the discussion, the WG agreed the draft was ready to move to formal discussion.
